How to make Banana Bread

  1. Mash 3 ripe bananas and mix with melted butter, sugar, an egg, and vanilla extract
  2. Stir in flour, baking soda, and a pinch of salt.
    Do this with love
  3. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan and bake at 350°F (175°C) for about an hour

Ingredients for Banana Bread

  • 3 Ripe Bananas
  • Flour
  • Baking Soda
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla Extract
  • salt
