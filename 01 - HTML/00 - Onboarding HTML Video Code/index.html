Letter for the Big Chungus

From Quandale

Oh Big Chungus , my swole wholey guacamole friend . It is of my greatest dismay to impart upon you such terrible news. Our dearest friend Amongus has sadly passed away. The details at the moment are yet to be disclosed. I sent this message in honor of your friendship with the freshly deceased .

You will receive a follow-up message in a few days regarding details on the funeral.
